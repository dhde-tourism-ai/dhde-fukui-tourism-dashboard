Reading note: this brief blends two official series that measure related but different things. Visitor Arrivals (JNTO's tourism-market estimate) drives the arrivals trend, seasonality, purpose-of-visit and share-of-market charts. Foreigners Entries (Ministry of Justice immigration counts, which include residents, workers and students, not just tourists) drives the age/gender, gender-trend and ports-of-entry charts. The two are never summed together, and 2026 figures throughout are partial-year (through May).
